A first person shooter where every character is steered by **two people on
two computers**. One of you has only the mouse, the other only the keyboard.
You both see through the same eyes.

- **Mouse:** look around, aim and shoot (left click)
- **Keyboard:** walk with **WASD**, jump with **Space**, reload with **R**
- When the mouse player turns around, "forward" turns too. Talk to each
  other, or don't, and enjoy the chaos.

**The fight:** every team against every other team in a small arena full of
crates, walls and pillars. Each character has **5 lives**, one hit costs one.
The pistol holds **5 shots**, needs **1 second** between shots and only gets
reloaded when the keyboard player presses R (2 seconds). After 20 seconds the
walls start closing in, until only a small square in the middle is left. The
last team standing wins.

**Players:** 2, 4, 6 or 8, started from a party in the launcher. Every team
needs a mouse and a keyboard player. With exactly 2 of you, you team up
against a bot.
