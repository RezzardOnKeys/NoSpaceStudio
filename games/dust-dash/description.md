A cosy race with vacuum cleaners in an old wooden hall. Two players clean
up dust, dirt and sawdust for four minutes. Whoever has vacuumed up the
most wins.

**This game is still being built.** The menu and the party connection are
in, the hall and the vacuuming follow.

Pick one of eight colours before the match.

**Play with a friend:** start a party in the launcher and choose this game,
you are connected right away for a 1 vs 1. **Play alone:** race against a
bot.
