A cosy race with vacuum cleaners in an old wooden hall. The floor is
covered with dust bunnies, dirt crumbs and wood shavings. You have four
minutes. Whoever has vacuumed up the most wins.

- **Vacuum.** Hold the **left mouse button** and walk over the dirt. Every
  piece flies into your nozzle and counts one point, and the warm wooden
  floor shows where you have been. What you have taken, your opponent
  cannot get any more.
- **Choose.** You walk slower while you vacuum. Take the dirt on the way,
  or let go and run?
- **Deliveries.** About every 35 seconds a cardboard box drops through a
  hatch in the roof. You see its icon through walls and shelves. Whoever
  touches it first gets a surprise for ten seconds: a **wide nozzle**,
  **turbo shoes**, **strong suction** or the **golden nozzle** that counts
  every piece twice.
- **Nobody gets hurt.** You cannot push or block each other. The only
  contest is the dirt.

**Controls:** **WASD** walk, **mouse** look around (click the game to catch
the mouse), hold **left click** to vacuum. Without a mouse the arrow keys
turn the view and **E** vacuums.

Pick one of eight colours before the match.

**Play with a friend:** start a party in the launcher and choose this game,
you are connected right away for a 1 vs 1. **Play alone:** race against a
bot that plays by the same rules as you.
