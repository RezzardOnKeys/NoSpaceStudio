A touch-typing trainer that turns your speed into a race.

- **Type to play the video.** Load any YouTube video (or your own MP4). Every
  correct keystroke moves it forward. Type at your goal speed and it plays in
  real time; stop typing and it stops too.
- **No deleting.** Every keystroke counts, mistakes stay visible.
- **Race a friend.** Start a party in the launcher, pick this game and you
  both get the same words. After each round you see who typed faster.
- **Learn the fingers.** An on-screen keyboard shows which finger to use,
  for QWERTZ and QWERTY.
- German and English word lists, rounds of 30 seconds to 2 minutes.
