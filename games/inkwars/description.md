A 3D water fight in a toy studio. Two sides spray the arena with their
colour. After three minutes the side that painted more ground wins.

- **Spray.** Aim with the mouse and hold the **left mouse button**. Every
  drop leaves your colour on the floor and on the walls.
- **Dive.** Hold **Shift** to turn into a fish. In your own colour you are
  fast, your opponent cannot see you, and your tank fills up again. You can
  even swim up walls you have painted. On dry ground a fish only flops.
- **Soak.** Hit your opponent often enough and they are out for three
  seconds, then they start again at their base. Your health comes back when
  you stay out of the water for a moment.
- **Watch out.** In your opponent's colour you are slow.

**Controls:** **WASD** move, **mouse** aim, **left click** spray, **Space**
jump, **Shift** fish form. Without a mouse the arrow keys turn the view and
**E** sprays.

Pick one of eight water colours before the match.

**Play with a friend:** start a party in the launcher and choose this game,
you are connected right away for a 1 vs 1. **Play alone:** fight bots,
1 v 1 or 2 v 2 with a bot on your side.
