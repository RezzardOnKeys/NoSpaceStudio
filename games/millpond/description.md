Nine Men's Morris, one of the oldest board games there is, played on a
wooden board on a cosy little island in a mill pond. Ladybugs against snails, koi swimming around the
island and a water mill turning its wheel at the shore.

- **Place, move, fly.** First take turns setting your nine pieces on the
  board. Then slide them along the lines, one step at a time. With only
  three pieces left, you may jump to any free point.
- **Close a mill.** Three of your pieces in a row on one line make a mill:
  the pieces light up, the water mill spins, and you take one piece of the
  other side. Pieces in a mill are safe, unless all of them stand in mills.
- **Win** when the other side has only two pieces left or cannot move. The
  same position three times, or fifty moves without a mill, is a draw.
- **Removed pieces leave in character.** A ladybug opens its wings and
  flies away; a snail slides into the pond with a splash, and the koi come
  to look.
- **Look around.** Turn and zoom the camera round the island, press **C**
  for your side and **N** for dusk with glowing lanterns and fireflies.
  Click the water and the koi swim over.

**Controls:** click a free point to set a piece; click your piece, then a
dot to move it. **Drag** turns the camera, the **wheel** zooms. **Resign**
by clicking the button twice.

**Play with a friend:** start a party in the launcher and choose this game,
you are connected right away for a 1 vs 1. The host picks the sides, and a
rematch swaps them. **Play alone:** against the computer, easy, medium or
hard, as Ladybugs, Snails or a random side. Alone you can also take moves
back.
