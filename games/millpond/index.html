How to play
  • Each side has 9 pieces. The Ladybugs begin.
  • First take turns setting your pieces on free points of the board.
  • When all are set, take turns sliding one piece along a line to the next free point.
  • Three of your pieces in a row on one line make a mill. Then you take one piece of the other side, but not one that stands in a mill, unless all of them do.
  • With only 3 pieces left you may jump to any free point.
  • You win when the other side has only 2 pieces left or cannot move.
  • It is a draw when the same position comes up three times, or after 50 moves in a row without a mill.
  • Click a point to set a piece · click your piece, then a dot to move
  • Drag: turn the camera · wheel: zoom · C: your side
