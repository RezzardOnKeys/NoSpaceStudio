Classic chess on a raised board in the middle of a floating island made
of blocks, with trees, flowers, torches and drifting clouds. Every piece is
a little block figure of its own that walks to its square, and a captured
piece vanishes in a puff of blocks.

- **Two armies.** The **Village Guard** (White) brings villagers, stone
  golems, lantern monks, knights on block horses, a crystal mage and the
  village elder. The **Cave Folk** (Black) answer with bone warriors,
  obsidian colossi, mushroom shamans, spider riders, a shadow witch and
  the cave king. Point at a piece to see its name.
- **All the rules.** Castling, en passant, promotion (you choose the new
  piece), check, checkmate, stalemate and every kind of draw.
- **Look around.** Turn, slide and zoom the camera, or fly across the
  island. Press **C** to swing back to your side and **N** for night with
  glowing torches and stars.
- **Resign** if a game is lost: click the button twice.

**Controls:** click a piece, then a square (green dots: moves, red: a
capture). **Drag** turns the camera, **right drag** slides it, the **wheel**
zooms, **WASD** flies, **Q/E** goes down and up.

**Play with a friend:** start a party in the launcher and choose this game,
you are connected right away for a 1 vs 1. The host picks the sides, and a
rematch swaps them. **Play alone:** against the computer, easy, medium or
hard, as White, Black or a random side. Alone you can also take moves back.
