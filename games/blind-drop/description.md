A comic-style bomb duel on a floating island in space. You get one bomb per
round, and you cannot see where your opponent goes.

- **Look (3 seconds).** Both players are visible. Nobody can move.
- **Go (5 seconds).** Your opponent turns invisible. Run with **WASD** and
  drop your bomb once with a **left click**. A circle shows where it will
  land and how big the blast is. Once it is set, it stays there.
- **Boom.** Everybody is revealed and all bombs fall at the same time.
  Hit your opponent and stay out of every blast to win. Your own bomb counts
  too, so do not stand in your own circle.
- **Nobody hit?** The island shrinks and the next round starts. It gets
  crowded fast.

Pick one of eight colours for your character and your explosion.

**Play with a friend:** start a party in the launcher and choose this game,
you are connected right away. **Play alone:** practice against a bot.
