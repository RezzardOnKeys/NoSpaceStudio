A one-on-one sniper duel in a walled Japanese temple garden. Each of you
has one shot, then the rifle needs exactly one second to reload. Every hit
wins the round. But every shot that misses throws the shooter to a
completely random spot in the garden: maybe behind the opponent, maybe on
the pagoda balcony, maybe right in front of their scope.

- **One shot, one second.** A hit lands the moment you pull the trigger,
  wherever it hits. There is no protection after landing and no time
  limit.
- **Zoom or gamble.** Hold the **right mouse button** to zoom in: the shot
  goes exactly where you aim, but you move slower. Shooting without zoom is
  allowed and very inaccurate.
- **Teleports you can see.** A soft column of light in the player's colour
  shows where someone teleported from and where they landed, so both of
  you know something just happened.
- **The temple garden:** an open gravel garden with large rocks in the
  middle, stone lanterns, cherry trees, bamboo, a tea house, a koi pond
  with a red bridge, a hill with a bell frame and a three-storey pagoda
  with a balcony.
- **First to 3** won rounds wins the match. Players swap sides every round.

**Controls:** **WASD** move, **mouse** look (click the game to catch the
mouse), hold **right click** to zoom, **left click** to shoot, **Space**
jump, **Shift** or **C** crouch (while running: slide). Without a mouse the
arrow keys turn the view, **Q** zooms and **E** shoots. The mouse speed can
be set in the menu.

Pick one of six coat colours before the match.

**Play with a friend:** start a party in the launcher and choose this game
for a 1 vs 1. **Play alone:** duel a bot that plays by the same rules as
you, misses sometimes and teleports too.
