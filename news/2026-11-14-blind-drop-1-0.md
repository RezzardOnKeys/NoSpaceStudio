Our second game is here: a 3D comic-style bomb duel for two players.

- **Look, then guess:** for 3 seconds you see your opponent, then they turn
  invisible. You have 5 seconds to run (WASD) and drop one bomb (left click)
- **Boom:** all bombs fall at the same time. Hit your opponent, but stay out
  of every blast, including your own
- **Nobody hit?** The island shrinks and the next round starts
- Eight colours for your character and your explosion
- **1 vs 1** from a party in the launcher, or **practice against a bot** when
  you start the game alone
