- **Party pictures:** your party is shown as overlapping profile pictures at
  the bottom left. A green ring means your opponent is ready for the next
  round. The panel at the top left is gone.
- **Video controls moved** to the bottom right: paste a YouTube link, load
  it, use your own file, switch the sound on or off. Nothing covers the
  video anymore.
- **Rematch with new settings:** after a round you return to the lobby
  first. The host can change the time, the goal speed and the video, then
  starts the next round with the new "Start round" button.
