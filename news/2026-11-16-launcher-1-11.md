- **Talk to your party:** the Party page has a chat below the members. New
  messages show up right away, press Enter to send.
- Only the members of the party read the chat, and only the messages
  written since they joined.
- Messages are deleted when the party ends, and after 24 hours at the
  latest in a party that runs longer.
- Up to 500 characters per message. Please do not share personal data in
  the chat.
