title: Inkwars 1.2.1 – colour only where water lands
type: game_update
date: 2026-11-16T12:23:00+01:00
game: inkwars
version: 1.2.1
---

- **Drips you can see:** small drips now fall out of your stream and paint
  the floor where they land. Before, the trail appeared on the floor under
  a shot even when the water was high up in the air
- **No more gaps:** a shot fired high no longer leaves a trail that stops
  in the middle. The line from your feet to the splat is always complete,
  it just fills in a moment later the higher the water flies
- You can still dive and swim right behind your shot at full speed
