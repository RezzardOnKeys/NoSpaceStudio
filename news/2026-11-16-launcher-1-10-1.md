---
title: A faster start
type: launcher_update
date: 2026-11-16T19:38:00+01:00
version: 1.10.1
---

- **Faster first visit:** pages are now loaded the first time you open
  them instead of all at once. The store shows up sooner, especially on
  phones.
- While a page loads for the first time you may see a grey placeholder for
  a split second.
- **Smooth updates:** if a new version goes live while the launcher is
  open, it reloads itself once the next time you open a new page.
