- **Faster water:** your drops fly about a third faster and reach about
  as far as before
- **Wider trail:** the trail behind a shot is half as wide again, so it is
  easier to swim right behind it
- **Clear knockouts:** a soaked player now swells up with your colour,
  bursts in a big splash and leaves their cap on the floor until they are
  back. "SOAKED!" rises above the spot, and you see "Opponent soaked!"
