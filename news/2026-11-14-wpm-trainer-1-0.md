The first game in the store, made by Balls.

- Type to drive a YouTube video (or your own MP4): every correct keystroke
  moves it forward
- German and English word lists, QWERTZ and QWERTY, rounds of 30 seconds to
  2 minutes, an on-screen keyboard that shows the right finger
- **Race a friend:** start it from a party and you are connected right away,
  with the same words for both of you
- Your playtime counts in the launcher
