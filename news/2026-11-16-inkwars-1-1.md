---

Inkwars now feels much more direct, alone and with a friend.

- **No more sliding:** on foot you stop and turn almost at once. The fish
  is as fast as before in your own colour, but only glides on briefly, and
  a leap out of your water no longer skids across dry ground
- **Better control in the air:** you can steer a jump much more
- **Swim behind your shot:** every shot leaves a trail without gaps that
  starts right at your feet and runs straight ahead. Shoot, hold Shift and
  W, and you swim along it at full speed
- **Shots land at the crosshair:** before, every shot landed a little to
  the left of it, and far shots fell short. Shots also spread much less
- **Party games:** your own character now moves the moment you press a
  key, also when you are not the host. Hits count where you saw your
  opponent on your screen, even with a slow connection
