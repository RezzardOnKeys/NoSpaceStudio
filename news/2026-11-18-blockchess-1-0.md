Our sixth game is here: classic chess on a raised board in the middle of a
floating island made of blocks.

- **Two armies of our own:** the Village Guard against the Cave Folk, twelve
  hand-made block figures that walk to their squares. Point at one to see
  its name
- **All the rules:** castling, en passant, promotion with a choice of
  piece, checkmate, stalemate and every kind of draw
- **Resign** with two clicks when a game is lost
- **Look around:** turn, zoom and fly the camera, **C** for your side,
  **N** for night with glowing torches
- **1 vs 1** from a party in the launcher (the host picks the sides, a
  rematch swaps them), or **alone against the computer** (easy, medium or
  hard, with taking moves back)
- A guest who reloads is back in the same game right away
- No sound, like all our games for now
