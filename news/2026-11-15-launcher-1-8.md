---
title: Games start from their page
type: launcher_update
date: 2026-11-15T12:55:00+01:00
version: 1.8.0
---

- **One place to start a game:** the store and the library now open the
  game's page. The play buttons on covers, cards and lists are gone, so
  nobody starts a game alone by accident while in a party.
- **In a party you play together:** the host only gets "Play with party".
  Members see a grey button with "Only the party host can start games."
  If the party is too big for a game, or the game is single player only,
  the button is grey and says why.
- While you play a party game, the party's pictures are shown in the bar
  at the top.
