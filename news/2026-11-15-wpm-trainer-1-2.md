- **New party panel** at the top left: both player names, who hosts, and
  both typing speeds live while you race. Bigger and easier to read.
- **Room codes are gone.** You no longer create a room or type a code: start
  a party in the launcher and you are connected right away. Started alone,
  the game is simply single player.
