Our fourth game is now open for everyone: Inkwars, a 3D water fight where
the ground you paint is your score.

- **Spray your colour:** after three minutes the side with more painted
  ground wins
- **Fish form:** hold Shift to dive into your own colour, move fast and
  unseen, and refill your tank
- **1 vs 1** from a party, or **alone against bots** (1 v 1 or 2 v 2)
- All patch notes so far are on the game's page
- No sound yet; it comes later
