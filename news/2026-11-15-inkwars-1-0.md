Our fourth game is here: a 3D water fight where the ground you paint is
your score.

- **Spray your colour:** every drop paints the floor and the walls. After
  three minutes the side with more painted ground wins
- **Fish form:** hold Shift to dive into your own colour. You are fast and
  invisible there, your tank refills, and you can swim up painted walls
- **Soak your opponent:** enough hits send them back to their base for
  three seconds
- Eight water colours to choose from
- **1 vs 1** from a party in the launcher, or **alone against bots**
  (1 v 1 or 2 v 2)
- No sound yet
