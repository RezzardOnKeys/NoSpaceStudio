- **Video controls at the top:** link field, "Load video", "Own file", sound
  and the video speed now sit in one thin bar above the video.
- **Party pictures** are shown by the launcher in the bar at the very top,
  next to the game's name. The pictures at the bottom left are gone.
- **Party status in words:** the top left says "Waiting for the other
  player…", "Connected with …" or "… is ready".
