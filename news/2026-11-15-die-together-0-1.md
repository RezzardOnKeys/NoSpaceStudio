Our third game is in testing: a comic first person shooter where every
character is steered by two people.

- **One mouse, one keyboard:** one of you looks, aims and shoots, the other
  walks, jumps and reloads. You both see through the same eyes
- **5 lives, 5 shots:** one hit costs one life, and the pistol only gets
  reloaded when the keyboard player presses R
- **Closing walls:** after 20 seconds the arena shrinks until only a small
  square in the middle is left. Obstacles sink into the floor before the
  walls reach them
- **2, 4, 6 or 8 players** from a party. With exactly 2 of you, you fight a
  bot together
- No sound yet; it comes later
