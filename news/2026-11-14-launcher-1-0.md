The first version of the launcher. Everything runs in your browser: no
downloads, no space needed.

- **Store** with live search, genre and engine filters and sorting
- **Game pages** with description, screenshots and patch notes
- **Player** that runs games right in the launcher, with fullscreen and a
  session timer
- **Library**, **news** and **profile** pages
- Dark design that works on laptops and phones
