Shooting feels like a real water pistol now.

- **A clean jet:** your water flies as smooth drops along its path, with
  no rain of small drips underneath any more
- **Colour where the water lands:** every drop leaves a splat where it
  comes down, a little longer in the direction it flew. A shot high into
  the air only paints where it lands, with no line on the floor below it
- **Grazing water paints:** water that flies low over the floor colours
  the ground right under it
- **Dive right in:** spray the floor in front of you, hold Shift and you
  are in your own colour at once
- Speed, range and aiming stay as in 1.2
