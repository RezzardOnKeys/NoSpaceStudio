Our new game is a sniper duel in a walled Japanese temple garden. Each of
you has one shot, and every shot that misses sends the shooter somewhere
completely random.

- **One shot, one second:** after every shot the rifle needs exactly one
  second to reload. Every hit wins the round, wherever it lands
- **Every miss teleports you:** maybe behind your opponent, maybe onto the
  pagoda balcony, maybe right in front of their scope. A column of light
  shows where you vanished and where you landed
- **Zoom or gamble:** zoomed in, the shot goes exactly where you aim, but
  you move slower. Without zoom it goes somewhere near the crosshair
- **Quick on your feet:** run, jump, crouch behind the low stone walls and
  slide when you crouch while running
- **The temple garden:** raked gravel, cherry trees, bamboo, a tea house, a
  koi pond with a red bridge, a hill with a bell frame and a three-storey
  pagoda
- **First to 3** won rounds wins. A short slow motion shows every hit
- **1 vs 1** from a party in the launcher, or **alone against a bot** that
  plays by the same rules
- Six coat colours and a mouse speed slider in the menu
- No sound yet
