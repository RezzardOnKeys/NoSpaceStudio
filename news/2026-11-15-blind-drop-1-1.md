- **Blast size:** before a match the host picks **Small**, **Medium** or
  **Large**. Medium is the new default; Large is the size from 1.0.
- Smaller blasts make dodging easier, so the island now shrinks a little
  further for them. Every match still comes to an end.
- When the host changes the size, the other player confirms "Ready" again,
  so nobody starts with a size they did not see.
- In practice mode you choose the size yourself.
