A small fix for aiming:

- **Looking above the middle no longer fires the water sky high.** Before,
  a crosshair on the horizon or in the air made the gun lob the water
  steeply upwards. Now a shot leaves the gun at most a little above where
  the crosshair looks, just like when you aim at the floor
- Aiming at the floor and at walls works exactly as before
