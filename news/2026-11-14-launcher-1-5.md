- **Party** has its own place in the sidebar. While you are in a party, the
  members' pictures are shown next to it on every page.
- Every player is marked as **host** or **member**. The host can remove
  members and make someone else the host.
- The store shows every game the same way: one grid of equally sized covers.
- "Play alone" next to "Play with party" is now a proper button.
