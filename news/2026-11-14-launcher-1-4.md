The biggest update so far.

- **Playtime is saved** while you play. Your library, "Continue playing",
  your profile statistics and the "Most played" sorting now use real numbers.
- **Parties:** invite friends to a party. When the host starts a multiplayer
  game, everybody in the party joins it automatically.
- Single player games tell you that your party will not join you.
- The first game is in the store: **99-Finger WPM Trainer**.
