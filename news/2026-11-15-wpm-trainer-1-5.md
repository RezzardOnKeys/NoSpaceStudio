- **Party pictures at the top left:** your party is shown as overlapping
  profile pictures in the bar at the top, next to the video controls. They
  replace the status text.
- A **dashed circle** means the other player has not joined yet. A **green
  ring** tells the host that the opponent is ready for the next round.
  Hover a picture to see the name.
