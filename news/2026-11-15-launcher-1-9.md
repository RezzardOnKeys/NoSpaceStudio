---
title: Player counts for party games
type: launcher_update
date: 2026-11-15T13:53:00+01:00
version: 1.9.0
---

- **Games can need a party:** a game can now ask for a minimum number of
  players and for an even number of players. Store cards show the real
  range, for example "2–8".
- The game page explains the rules under "Players", for example "2–8, even
  number, party only".
- **The play button says why it is grey:** without a party you see "Needs a
  party of 2, 4, 6 or 8 players". As host you see "Needs at least …" when
  the party is too small, or "Needs an even number of players. Your party
  has 3." when one player is missing.
