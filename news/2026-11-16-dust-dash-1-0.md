Our fifth game is here: two players clean an old wooden hall, and whoever
has vacuumed up the most after four minutes wins.

- **Vacuum the hall:** more than eleven thousand dust bunnies, crumbs and
  wood shavings fly into your nozzle, one point each. Clean wood shows
  where you have been
- **Run or vacuum:** you are slower while you vacuum
- **Deliveries:** a box drops through the roof about every 35 seconds, at
  a spot that is equally far for both. Its icon shows through walls
- **Four surprises** for ten seconds: wide nozzle, turbo shoes, strong
  suction and the golden nozzle (double points)
- A new layout of the dirt in every match
- Eight colours to choose from
- **1 vs 1** from a party in the launcher, or **alone against a bot**
- Nobody can push or block anybody. The only contest is the dirt
- No sound yet
