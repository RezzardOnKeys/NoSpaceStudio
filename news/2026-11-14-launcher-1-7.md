- **Online status for friends:** a green dot means online, a violet dot means
  playing. Lists show "Online", "Playing …" or "Offline · last seen …".
- Friends who are online are listed first, also when you invite them to a
  party.
- The sidebar shows how many friends are online right now.
- **Imprint** and **privacy policy** are now real pages.
- To create an account you confirm that you are at least 16 years old.
