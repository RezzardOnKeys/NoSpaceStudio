When the time is up, there is now a moment to enjoy your work:

- **A camera flight through the hall:** for ten seconds the camera glides
  low along the sunny windows, rises over the middle and ends with a view
  over the whole hall. You see every clean lane the two of you left
- **How clean is it?** A label counts up how much of the hall is clean and
  how many pieces were vacuumed in this round
- Then the receipt with the result appears as before
- In a hurry? Click or press any key to skip the flight
- With "reduce motion" switched on in your system you see three still
  pictures instead of the flight
