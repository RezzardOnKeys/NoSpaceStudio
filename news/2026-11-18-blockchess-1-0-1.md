A fix for the end of a game, and a lighter load on your computer.

- **Fixed: the board could vanish** at the end of a game and leave only a
  blue screen. The pieces now take far less work to draw (about a ninth
  of before) and look exactly the same
- **If the 3D view ever drops out anyway**, the game brings it back by
  itself. If the browser does not allow that, a notice offers a reload,
  and your game goes on where it was
