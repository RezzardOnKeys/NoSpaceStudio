- **New mode: Snipe.** The blast is tiny and the island never shrinks. A
  match lasts until somebody lands a real hit, however long that takes.
- **A darker look for Snipe:** a night sky with a pale moon, a stone platform
  painted like the view through a scope, and a crosshair for aiming.
- The host picks the mode in the lobby: **Classic** (the game as you know
  it) or **Snipe**. In practice mode you choose yourself.
- The "BOOM!" is smaller for small blasts, so you can see where they hit.
