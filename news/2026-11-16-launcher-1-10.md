---
title: Online means really online
type: launcher_update
date: 2026-11-16T19:14:00+01:00
version: 1.10.0
---

- **Online only while you are here:** your friends see you as online while
  you really use the launcher or a game. A tab in the background, five
  minutes without mouse or keyboard, or a closed tab count as offline, and
  your friends see it at once instead of a minute later.
- **Parties tidy up themselves:** whoever is offline for more than three
  minutes leaves their party. If that was the host, the member who joined
  first takes over and a running game is stopped.
- **Invitations on every screen:** a new party invitation now floats in the
  top right corner, even while you play. Join, decline, or put it aside for
  later; then it waits at the top of the regular pages.
- **A crown for the new host:** when you become the party host, a small
  golden crown tells you, wherever you are.
