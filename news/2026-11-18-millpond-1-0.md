Our new game is a calm one for a cup of tea: Nine Men's Morris on a wooden
board on a little island in a mill pond, with ladybugs against snails.

- **Place, move, fly:** set your nine pieces, slide them along the lines,
  and jump anywhere once only three are left
- **Close a mill:** three in a row light up, the water mill spins its
  wheel, and you take a piece of the other side
- **Leaving in style:** a removed ladybug opens its wings and flies away; a
  removed snail slides into the pond with a splash, and the koi come to look
- **The mill pond:** koi in the water, water lilies, reeds, a water mill at
  the shore, and hills all around. Turn and zoom the camera as you like
- **Dusk:** press N for a warm evening with glowing stone lanterns and
  fireflies over the reeds
- **Click the water** and the koi swim over
- **1 vs 1** from a party in the launcher, or **alone against the
  computer** (easy, medium or hard), where you can also take moves back
- No sound yet
