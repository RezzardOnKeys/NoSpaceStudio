- **Smoother:** the waves and the lantern light at dusk now cost your
  computer much less work, so the pond runs more smoothly, especially on
  older laptops
- **The koi keep their distance from the water mill** and no longer brush
  the wheel
- **Take the camera at any time:** dragging right after a game starts now
  turns the camera at once, instead of fighting the swing to your side
- Fast clicking into the water no longer piles up endless rings
