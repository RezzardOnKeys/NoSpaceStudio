## 2. Overview: what we process

- **Email address and password:** for signing in and resetting your
  password. Nobody can see them; the password is only stored as a secure
  hash.
- **Username, profile picture and date of joining:** your public profile,
  visible to everybody.
- **Friends and friend requests:** visible to you and the other person.
- **Playtime per game:** for your library and statistics, visible to you and
  your confirmed friends.
- **Online status, current game and "last seen":** visible to your confirmed
  friends.
- **Party membership and invitations:** visible to the members of the party.
- **Technical data** (IP address, browser, time of access): needed to
  deliver the website and keep it secure, processed by our hosting
  providers.

We do not sell data, do not show advertising and do not create user
profiles for marketing.

## 3. Hosting (Cloudflare)

The website and the games are delivered by Cloudflare Pages (Cloudflare,

Cloudflare processes your IP address and technical information about your
browser to deliver the page and to protect it against attacks.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is a fast and
secure website. Cloudflare is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy

Accounts, profiles, friendships, playtime, parties, online status and

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR, because the data is needed to provide the
account and its features that you signed up for.

## 7. Friends, playtime and online status

- **Playtime** is saved while a game is open and your browser tab is
  visible, about every 30 seconds.
- **Online status:** while the launcher is open, it reports every 30 seconds
  that you are online and which game you are playing. From this, your
  friends see "Online", "Playing …" or "Offline · last seen …". There is no
  setting to hide your status; it is only visible to people you accepted as
  friends.
- A **total playtime per game** of all players combined is public, without
  any connection to individual players.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR.

## 8. Parties and multiplayer games

In a party, the members see each other and the game the party is playing.
When a multiplayer game starts, the players' browsers connect **directly to
each other** (WebRTC). For this, the other players receive your IP address;
this is technically necessary for a direct connection.

To find each other, games such as the 99-Finger WPM Trainer use the public
PeerJS server (0.peerjs.com, run by the open source PeerJS project). It
receives your IP address and a random connection ID, but no account data.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR.

## 9. YouTube videos in the 99-Finger WPM Trainer

In the 99-Finger WPM Trainer you can load a YouTube video. Only when you do
this, the video is loaded from YouTube (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House,
Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) in YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode.
YouTube then receives your IP address and information about your browser and
may store information on your device. Without loading a video, no connection
to YouTube is made.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR: you decide to load the video. More
information: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 10. Storage in your browser

We do not use tracking or advertising cookies. The website stores your login
session in your browser's local storage so that you stay signed in. Games
may store their settings and save games in your browser as well. This is
technically necessary for the services you use (§ 25 (2) TDDDG). You can
delete this data at any time in your browser settings.

Fonts are part of the website itself; no fonts are loaded from Google or
other providers.
